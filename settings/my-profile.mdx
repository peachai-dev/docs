The **My Profile** tab contains the basic information associated with your Peach account.

To access your profile, go to **Settings → My Profile**.

## Account information

Your profile displays:

- **Email address**: the email address associated with your Peach account
- **Name**: your name as it appears in Peach

## Report a bug

If you experience an issue while using Peach, select **Report a bug** to contact the Peach team.

When submitting a report, include as much information as possible about what you were doing and what happened.

<Info>
  The Peach team will respond to reported bugs within 24 hours.
</Info>

## Sign out

Select **Sign Out** to end your current Peach session.
