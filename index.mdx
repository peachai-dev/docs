Peach is an AI-powered communications platform built specifically for financial aid offices.

By connecting your shared inbox to Peach, your team can manage student inquiries, collaborate on responses, access institutional knowledge, and leverage AI to reduce repetitive work.

## Why Peach

Financial aid teams manage thousands of student questions each year, often across multiple staff members, inboxes, and systems.

Peach brings these workflows into a single platform, giving financial aid teams one place to manage student inquiries, collaborate across the office, access institutional and regulatory knowledge, and use AI to reduce repetitive work.

---

## Built for financial aid

Unlike general-purpose help desk tools, Peach is designed specifically for higher education financial aid offices.

Peach helps teams:

- Respond to student inquiries faster
- Maintain consistency across staff responses
- Reduce repetitive email drafting
- Preserve institutional knowledge
- Improve visibility into office performance

## How Peach works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your shared mailbox">
    Connect your financial aid inbox to Peach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive student inquiries">
    Incoming emails are automatically imported into Peach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work tickets collaboratively">
    Staff can review, assign, and respond to student questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send responses">
    Messages are sent directly from your institution's mailbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>
